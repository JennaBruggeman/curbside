The fifteen checks

Every design is checked against the City of Vancouver Parklet Manual's siting criteria.

  1. C01 All-day parking permitted (no rush-hour, bus, taxi, or loading zone)
  2. C02 Adjacent travel lane ≥ 3.0 m (3.2 m for bus/truck routes)
  3. C03 Minimum 6 m setback from nearest intersection / crosswalk edge
  4. C04 Street running slope ≤ 5%
  5. C05 Minimum 5 m clearance on either side of any fire hydrant
  6. C06 Minimum 1.5 m setback from adjacent driveways or lanes
  7. C07 Minimum 1 m clearance from poles (2.4 m if trolley wires attached)
  8. C08 Minimum 2 m clearance from traffic signal controller boxes / electrical kiosks
  9. C09 Minimum 1 m clearance from manhole lids, valves, grates, access chambers
  10. C10 Does not block Fire Dept. connections, utility connections, or fire exits
  11. C11 Curb and roadside drainage maintained — street drains not blocked
  12. C12 Minimum pedestrian clearances on boulevard / utility strip maintained
  13. C13 Minimum 1.5 m setback from adjacent parking spaces
  14. C14 Platform flush with sidewalk (max 12 mm gap; max 13 mm connector)
  15. C15 Platform load capacity ≥ 7.2 kPa
